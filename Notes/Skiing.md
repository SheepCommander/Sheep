POP:
- When your toes (of your boots) hit the end of the lip
- Using your ankles, stacked under your shoulders, and bringing your knees up to your chest

ANKLES: Always bending! If your skis aren't centered right underneath, you're only half a spring! (knees and butt VS ankles flexing!)

POP EARLIER! Also: spring up, EXTEND full straight into the air (*then* pull your knees up) on pop.

DOLPHIN TURNS! Tips hit first (always forward/centered over skis): push tips down, or pull heels up? Both?
- Pop up, start pivot (flick down), then hit ground and turn. (Not turning before, and don't have to airplane turn unless necessary either)
