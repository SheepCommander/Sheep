Prevent:
1. Pull back the bedsheets and check the corners
2. Don't set suitcase on bed or floor: set on luggage rack/bath tub
3. Don't put clothes in drawers or lying around, keep in luggage
Defense: 
4. Mattress encasements
5. Wash & Dry clothes/sheets once a week, on highest heat
6. Simplify room, put clutter (especially dirty clothes) into plastic bag/tote
	1. Move bed away from wall
Offense: 
7. Vacuum, empty into bag
8. Diatomaceous Earth (Food Grade) (Light dusting, not clumps) (Power outlets, penetrating cracks)
9. 122F / 50C steamer
	1. Or put in freezer for 3 days if can't wash/dry
	2. Or heat entire house to 122F for 8 hours with a professional

Finally: 
1. Stick traps under bed legs so you can be sure they're totally gone!
